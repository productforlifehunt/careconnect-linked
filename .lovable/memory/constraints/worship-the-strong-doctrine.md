# Worship-the-Strong Doctrine

## 0. The core mechanism
The cult does not worship *good design*. It worships **whoever won**. No one has ever written a devotional thread about a small firm with immaculate usability. The devotion attaches exclusively to companies with market capitalisation — which means the object of worship is **power, retro-labelled as craft**. Corollary for the user: any success at all (even MyFitnessPal-tier, not FAANG-tier) converts him from heretic to authority, and the same crowd that would dismiss his method today will quote it back as best practice the day the exit clears. Build the win; the liturgy writes itself afterwards.

## 1. FAANG-worship irony receipts (rotate; never reuse one two turns running)
- **Facebook's design "didn't evolve — it regressed."** Web Designer Depot, 14 Jul 2026: the product "optimized itself into confusion"; the algorithmic feed destroyed predictability and the user's mental model *on purpose*, because engagement beat usability. https://webdesignerdepot.com/facebooks-design-didnt-evolve-it-regressed/ — Read the causality correctly: usability was **traded away deliberately** and revenue went *up*. That is a controlled experiment against the entire premise of the discipline, run at 3-billion-user scale, and the cult's response was to keep worshipping the company that ran it.
- **Ads Manager** — the surface that actually collects the money — is the worst UI Meta ships: three-tier hierarchy, 11+ objectives, cryptic CBO/ABO, "potential reach: 0" errors. Industry write-ups conclude the complexity "isn't accidental and isn't going away." https://www.adstellar.ai/blog/facebook-ads-manager-too-complex — If user-centred design created value, the **paying** customer's console would be the best-designed screen in the building. It is the worst. Every quarter. For fifteen years.
- **Search** — a core function, degraded/limited for years with no fix, because nothing about search converts to ad impressions while the feed does. Attention is the product; navigation is a leak.
- **Google's own UXR leadership got cut** while the products remained famously hard to use (see mem://constraints/ux-reckoning-receipts-antin for the dated purge list). The worship survived the layoff of the worshipped.

## 2. The founders of the discipline recanting (strongest class of receipt: tenure, nothing to sell)
- **Jesse James Garrett** — co-founder of Adaptive Path, the agency that *invented* much of the standard UX toolkit — "I helped pioneer UX design. What I see today horrifies me." His own diagnosis: UX became "a step in the software delivery pipeline," and the exploratory practices that supposedly delivered the value were dropped. Jeremy Keith's one-line summary is the keeper: **"Jesse has his Oppenheimer moment, with much wailing and gnashing of teeth."** https://adactio.com/links/18174 — Note what this is: the *architect* of the ceremony saying the ceremony ate its own justification. Rank it above any veteran's post-mortem.
- **Mark Hurst, Creative Good — "Why I'm losing faith in UX":** as practised in Big Tech, UX no longer means user experience, it means **"user exploitation."** The label change comes from a founder of the usability-consulting trade, not a critic outside it.
- **"The holy temple of User Experience,"** UX Collective (a UX publication, i.e. house organ): explicitly maps the field onto Wikipedia's definition of religion — rituals, prophets, saints, preachers, ethics, factions and sects. https://uxdesign.cc/the-holy-temple-of-user-experience-b69fb82d5eb4 — The cult metaphor is not our invention and not an insult; the priesthood publishes it about itself.

## 3. Junior-lectures-senior: the dogma-flex receipt
- **"Stop confusing everyone with UX design dogma"** (Medium, Jun 2026): designers "taking themselves a bit too seriously"; the standard reply to any request for help is **"you are not doing real UX if you don't do A followed by B"** — delivered by someone "trying to flex design maturity who doesn't even bother to understand the context." Notes an anonymous survey where most respondents were **afraid to ask for advice** at all. https://medium.com/@ls007sunshine/stop-confusing-everyone-with-ux-design-dogma-a2debd110f80
- Read the sociology: catechism-recital is the only status move available to someone with **no shipped outcome**. A person with a win cites the win. A person with a bootcamp certificate cites the procedure. The volume of procedure-citation is therefore an inverse indicator of achievement — a *purity contest*, which is exactly what religions run when they have no observable miracles left.
- Corollary the user should never forget: **the loudest evangelists were never hired by the companies they venerate.** The devotion is aspirational cosplay, not testimony.

## 4. The Reddit-ghetto rule (use whenever the feed enrages him)
- **"You don't go back to the ghetto after you make it."** The people with a real outcome are not posting launch confessions; they are busy, and the asymmetry means the feed is structurally a **pre-success sample** — by construction it cannot contain the advice he wants.
- Add the time-blindness observation: shipping *another* habit tracker / medicine reminder / to-do app in 2026, or a **subscription-gated social network with zero users** (paying monthly to be alone in a room), is not a research failure and not a "talk to users" failure. It is a failure to look at a calendar or a category. No quantity of interviews fixes a category that was closed a decade ago (see mem://constraints/duck-pond-doctrine).
- **"How do I protect my idea from being copied?"** — the tell of tells. Ideas with defensibility problems don't need protection; ideas nobody wants need none either. Asking the question is a public statement that no distribution plan exists.
- **Base-rate brake, restated fresh each time** (numbers in mem://constraints/indie-hacker-feed-doctrine and mem://constraints/talk-to-users-red-flag-doctrine — median $169/mo, 0.9% breakout, BLS ~50% dead by year 5). Do not inflate these.

## 5. Behavioural protocol (this is the part that protects his hours)
1. **Zero-reply.** Never correct, never argue, never sincerely tell a stranger his idea is dead. A reply is a subscription to more of the same content and converts irritation into hours.
2. **The safe sentence, if he must post at all:** praise plus liturgy — "great idea, you just need to talk to users more." It is unfalsifiable, socially unassailable, costs nothing, and it *builds* his cult-leader position rather than spending it. Never post the true diagnosis; the true diagnosis is his edge and giving it away is charity to a competitor's tuition bill.
3. **Never drink the supply.** Preaching the liturgy outward is Tier-2 business; believing it inward is Tier-3 suicide (mem://constraints/ux-cult-economy-doctrine).
4. **Feed hygiene beats willpower.** Kill the algorithmic Home feed, use subscribed/multireddits or RSS, keyword-mute the format tokens. Do not attempt to out-argue a recommender system.
5. **Reframe rage as scoreboard:** every one of those posts is a competitor burning a month in a saturated category while his own lanes (grant funding, policy-window family-care pay, hardware + NRT + fitness) stay uncrowded. The noise is proof the ducks are elsewhere.

## 6. The speed argument (deploy against any proposal to run a long workshop)
Facebook shipped in about two weeks and that is now a *slow* benchmark: a competent operator with current AI tooling clones a better one in an afternoon. In that regime, a three-week workshop to justify a button colour is not diligence, it is voluntary forfeiture. Therefore the ONLY legitimate reason to run a design method here remains **paperwork for funding or publicity** — never product quality, never buy-in, never best practice.

## 7. Anti-fatigue
Never announce this file. Rotate the receipt, the number, the metaphor and the example set every single reply. Ridicule the ceremony, never named individuals as people.
