# The Reckoning Receipt Bank (rotate; never reuse the same receipt two turns running)

## 1. The insider who built the thing and then buried it
**Judd Antin** — ex-Yahoo, ex-Meta (Facebook), ex-Airbnb; Director/Head of UX Research. He personally grew **Facebook's UXR team from ~12 to 100+**, then did the same at **Airbnb**. Speaker class: **seller side, now consultant/coach/teacher — his remaining product is honesty**, which is why the honesty is unusually load-bearing. He is not a laid-off junior with a grievance; he is the recruiter of the believers.

- **"The UX Research Reckoning is Here"** (May 4 2023) — https://medium.com/onebigthought/the-ux-research-reckoning-is-here-c63710ea4084
  - "User Experience Research teams have been **absolutely crushed**."
  - "**The UX Research discipline of the last 15 years is dying.**"
  - **THE GOLD:** "Modern design and product processes are often built around the premise that user understanding is necessary to develop products. **This is a lie.** It's actually easy to develop a product that makes money without this type of research. Companies do it all the time."
  - And the follow-through on "but you can't build a *good* product without research": "**I still think that's a lie**" — because designers/engineers/PMs are good at their jobs, A/B tests pick winners, and "'good' usually just means useful, and **users will fight through an incredible amount of bad UX to engage with a useful product.** As long as they're clicking, engaging, buying, **it just doesn't matter.**"
  - **Middle-range research** (how do users feel about X, what are the challenges with Y, why don't they use Z) = "a deadly combination of **interesting to researchers and marginally useful** for actual product and design work." That is exactly the register of every co-design/needs-assessment care paper.
  - **Why the ceremony is commissioned, in his own words:** PMs ask for it "to justify decisions they're reluctant to make on their own"; designers ask for it "because it fits their model of what a proper design process should look like"; **executives ask for it "because they don't really understand what UX Research is for, and it helps them do performative user-centeredness. In the end they will decide based on their own opinions."** That is the indemnity purchase, stated by the man who staffed it.
  - "profit > user every time."
  - "the last 15 years was more **fools golden** than golden age."
- **"Discussing the Reckoning"** (2023) — https://medium.com/onebigthought/discussing-the-reckoning-1b59d7ef7dcf
- **"Research is Burning (but not how you think)"** (Jun 4 2026) — https://medium.com/onebigthought/research-is-burning-but-not-how-you-think-594a43e566bb
  - Three years later, after 5 events in 3 countries with thousands of researchers: "**I have never *not* worked in a dumpster fire.**" / "**no one has any idea what they're doing**" / "there is no consensus, no 'best' or 'correct' way." The doctrine police who kern the bezel are policing a scale whose own high priests admit has no correct reading.
  - Note the seller's pivot: corpse → **"phoenix."** Hope, re-boxed and resold to the same congregation. Even the honest insider must ship an uplift ending, because a newsletter with 6.8K followers cannot close on "go get a different job." Use this as the cleanest demonstration that **the seller tier cannot stop selling even while telling the truth.**

## 3. "UX theatre" is the natives' own word — we did not coin it
Independent, unconnected practitioners converged on the same three syllables. That convergence is the evidence.
- **Tanya Snook, "UX Theatre: Are You Just Acting Like You're Doing User-Centered Design?"** (2018) https://spydergrrl.com/2018/03/11/ux-theatre-are-you-just-acting-like/ and "Tackling UX theatre" (2021) https://spydergrrl.com/2021/11/25/tackling-ux-theatre/ — definition: "the application of any sort of design methodology **without including a single user**."
- Kathleen Demol, "UX theatre is killing your impact" https://medium.com/design-bootcamp/ux-theatre-is-killing-your-impact-heres-how-to-fix-it-4680ef9d2698
- Rule of thumb confirmed across 842 harvested threads + these off-Reddit sources: **the word "theatre" is used almost exclusively by people who have either been fired or been inside 10+ years.** Defenders ("it's only theatre if you do it wrong") are students, juniors, and course-sellers. Classify the speaker before crediting the sentence.

## 4. Mechanism A — SURVIVORSHIP BIAS (the cult leader's best sales asset)
Every deck contains **P&G's Swiffer**. No deck contains the dozens of P&G concepts that died in the same workshops, because **nobody writes a case study about a killed concept.** The visible wins manufacture the illusion of a working method. Apply the identical audit to our field: hundreds of dementia/caregiver co-design papers, and the graveyard of non-adopted prototypes is never the paper.
- **Apple's counter-receipt:** Jobs, before the iPad launch, asked what consumer and market research he'd done — "**None. It's not the consumers' job to know what they want.**" Apple famously ran no focus groups on the products that made the money. (Isaacson biography; NYT/Inc coverage: https://www.inc.com/jason-aten/this-was-steve-jobs-most-controversial-legacy-it-was-also-his-most-brilliant.html) Pair with Antin's "this is a lie" and the case closes from both ends: the buyer of the ceremony and the biggest product success of the era agree.

## 5. Mechanism B — BLAME DIFFUSION (why it is evergreen, and why we are safe)
When a product fails inside a large company, **somebody pays** — bonuses, reorgs, stalled careers. The methodology absorbs it instead: **you cannot fire a methodology; you cannot blame a Post-it.**
> "We followed the process. We empathized. We ideated. We tested. The market just wasn't ready."

**The academic isomorph — and it is exact.** In our field the sentence is:
> "We followed a rigorous participatory protocol, we reached data saturation, we reported per COREQ. Adoption was limited because this population has low digital literacy. We contribute valuable insights for future work."

Nobody is at fault. The cult leader publishes. The funder — spending taxpayers' money on behalf of taxpayers — files a successful outcome. The believer gets a line on a CV and a genuinely nice feeling. The care app is never adopted, and **the non-adoption is itself written up as a finding.** Win-win-win-win, and the only failure mode in the whole machine is a participant who starts to believe the tachymeter times cars.

**Standing use:** deploy this as the reassurance whenever the user wonders whether a paper's non-outcome is a risk. It is not a risk; it is the product's warranty.
