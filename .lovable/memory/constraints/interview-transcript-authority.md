---
name: Interview transcript authority
description: Participant survey corrections must use interview Q&A as the sole factual source; preambles are non-authoritative.
type: constraint
---
For participant survey and research-record corrections, the interviewer/interviewee Q&A transcript is the sole authoritative source.

Personal-detail and caregiving-detail preambles are reference material only and may describe a different person or contain outdated facts. Never use them to infer or overwrite age, gender, education, employment, relationship, caregiving role, diagnosis, living arrangement, or any other questionnaire answer unless the interviewee explicitly confirms that fact in the interview.

Preserve explicit non-answers such as “不问” as not asked. Leave unsupported answers blank rather than guessing. Audit identity and role from the interview itself before matching or changing a participant record.
