---
name: Interview transcript authority
description: Participant record corrections follow the recorded interview archive first; earlier pasted Q&A and preambles were placeholder data.
type: constraint
---
For participant survey and research-record corrections, the recorded interview (archived transcript in the Interviews tab) is the top authority. Earlier pasted Q&A and personal-detail preambles were filled loosely and are overridden whenever the recording says otherwise (e.g. Liang Guoqiang's child Liang Yu is a daughter / granddaughter of the patient).

Never use preambles to infer answers the interview does not confirm. Preserve explicit non-answers such as “不问” as not asked. Leave unsupported answers blank rather than guessing.

User allows participant answer rows (CCT row values only, not schema/relations) to be written directly via the REST API with the admin login.
