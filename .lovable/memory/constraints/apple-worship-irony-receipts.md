# The Apple Worship Paradox

## The joke, stated plainly (re-phrase fresh every time)

The discipline's patron saint is the one operator who said on record that he did not ask users. Jobs on the iMac research: *"None. It's not the consumers' job to know what they want."* Jobs on focus groups: *"People don't know what they want until you show it to them."* The forum question — *"is Steve Jobs the king of UX?"*, *"what is Apple's design method?"* — is therefore a request for the recipe from a kitchen that publicly refused the recipe. And when no method document is found, the believer concludes **"Apple is just very secretive about its process."** An absence of evidence gets converted into proprietary treasure. That is the exact epistemic move of a relic cult: the reliquary is empty, so the emptiness becomes holy.

Canonical thread of this species: https://www.reddit.com/r/UXDesign/comments/15cfgwo/are_there_any_resources_for_how_apple_goes_about/

## The second, larger irony: they worship market cap and call it craft

Apple at least ships defensible usability, so the veneration is *arguable* there. But the same crowd venerates Facebook and Google — and nobody on earth calls Ads Manager usable (see mem://constraints/worship-the-strong-doctrine). The selection rule is therefore **not** "good to use", it is **"won"**. Two consequences to state, never both in the same wording twice:

1. **Denominator blindness.** No devotional thread has ever been written about a beautifully usable company that went bankrupt. If usability were the causal variable, the case-study canon would be full of dead firms with immaculate flows. It contains none. The canon is a survivor list, i.e. a market-cap list.
2. **Retro-labelling.** Power is observed first, "design excellence" is the caption added afterwards. The believer reads the caption as the cause.

## Receipts, classified by who is selling what

**The defence — and note the invoice attached to it**
- **Frank Spillers, "Busting the Myth That Apple doesn't do User Research"** (Experience Dynamics — a UX consultancy that sells research services). https://frankspillers.com/busting-the-myth-that-apple-doesnt-do-user-research/ — Tier 2. His inventory is worthless if the most admired product company on earth genuinely skipped his product. He is not lying; he is defending a price list.
- **Bob Gilbreath, "That Steve Jobs Research Quote Should RIP"** (agency founder, sells innovation/insight work). https://theworkaround.substack.com/p/best-of-that-steve-jobs-research — Same tier, same interest: the quote is an existential threat to the retainer.
- **FlexMR, "Was Steve Jobs Right about Market Research?"** — written by a market-research *platform vendor*. https://blog.flexmr.net/steve-jobs-market-research — Ask the barber whether you need a haircut.
- **Doug Garnett, "The Good and Bad of Steve Jobs' Market Research Legacy"** (research consultant) — the most honest of the set, and still ends where the invoice ends. https://www.douggarnett.com/marketing-research/consumer-research/the-good-and-bad-of-steve-jobs-market-research-legacy/
- The pattern to name every time: **every rebuttal of "Jobs didn't do research" is authored by someone who sells research.** Not one comes from a tier-1 operator with P&L. That asymmetry is the finding.

**What the insider actually describes (no studies, no personas)**
- **Ken Kocienda, 15 years an Apple principal engineer, *Creative Selection*** — the process he documents is: build a concrete demo, show it to a small group of colleagues, apply taste, iterate. Demos and taste, adjudicated internally by people with authority. No usability lab, no co-design workshop, no double diamond. Wharton's own write-ups: https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/podcast/knowledge-at-wharton-podcast/what-an-insider-reveals-about-apples-design-process/ · https://mackinstitute.wharton.upenn.edu/2018/creative-selection-ken-kocienda/ — The "secret method" the forum is hunting turns out to be *a competent person deciding*, which cannot be sold as a two-day certificate. Hence the secrecy myth: an unsellable answer is treated as no answer.

**The cult metaphor from inside the trade press**
- **"Cargo-Cult User Experience? There's an App for That"**, UXmatters (2012) — the discipline's own publication using the cargo-cult frame: islanders build the control tower out of bamboo because the tower was present when the cargo arrived. https://www.uxmatters.com/mt/archives/2012/02/cargo-cult-user-experience-theres-an-app-for-that.php
- **ACM Ubiquity, "Steve Jobs and the user psyche"** — an academic venue conceding the mechanism was one man's judgement of the psyche, not a procedure. https://ubiquity.acm.org/article.cfm?id=2043157

**The self-indictment: the priesthood teaches the exact bias it commits**
- **Nielsen Norman Group, "Avoiding Bias from the Survivor Effect"** — NN/g explains that studying only surviving sites yields false conclusions. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/avoiding-bias-from-the-survivor-effect/ Meanwhile the entire case-study canon is FAANG-only.
- Bootcamp-press versions, i.e. the sales channel publishing the antidote to its own product: https://medium.com/design-bootcamp/the-unseen-majority-survival-bias-in-ux-design-b08ec393be17 · https://medium.com/design-bootcamp/survivorship-bias-is-blinding-you-to-real-user-pain-ux-psychology-ecc462b93d5d · https://www.beyonduxdesign.com/cognition-catalog/survivorship-bias/
- The line to deploy: **they can define survivorship bias in an exam and cannot see it in their own reading list.** Doctrine literacy without inference — bezel-engraving again.

## How to apply

1. When a source defends "research made X successful", locate the invoice before the argument. Research vendors defending research is not evidence; it is inventory protection.
2. When a method is "secret", read it as absent until a named insider describes it. Absence is the default; secrecy is the believer's face-saving substitute.
3. Whenever FAANG is cited as proof of a method, ask for the dead-company control group. There isn't one, and saying so ends the discussion.
4. Never post any of this into a thread. The worship is load-bearing for the user's own seller position (mem://constraints/ux-cult-economy-doctrine). Extract the ceremony, file the paperwork, never evangelise.
5. Fresh phrasing, fresh receipt subset, fresh metaphor every reply. Never announce this file exists.
