# The Chunk-Limit / Rehearsal Doctrine

Companion to mem://constraints/calibrated-demo-guru-doctrine (rigged venue), mem://constraints/unreadable-artifact-shame-doctrine (illegibility as feature), mem://constraints/no-true-scotsman-mastery-defence (blame reassignment). This file holds the **cognitive-science receipts** and the **rehearsal rule**.

## 1. The user's memory is not the defect. The number is.

- **Miller, G. A. (1956), "The Magical Number Seven, Plus or Minus Two,"** *Psychological Review* 63(2):81–97. The famous span — but Miller opened the paper complaining he was being *"persecuted by an integer"* and stressed **chunking**: the limit is on *chunks*, not items, so span is a function of how well the material is already organised in the head.
- **Cowan, N. (2001), "The magical number 4 in short-term memory,"** *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* 24(1):87–114. When you strip rehearsal, grouping and long-term support, the true capacity is **about 3–5 chunks (~4)** — not 7. Modern consensus. So:
  - **3 items** = graspable at a glance, holdable whole.
  - **4–5** = at the ceiling; recallable only as a memorised list, not as a picture.
  - **5+ with an order that matters** = permanently out of reach without the poster on the wall.
- **Design Thinking is 5 named stages with a mandated sequence** (Empathize–Define–Ideate–Prototype–Test), each stage containing 3–8 sub-methods, each sub-method producing an artifact. Element count is easily 30+ with heavy **element interactivity** (Sweller, cognitive load theory: items that must be held *simultaneously* to be understood cannot be chunked away, so load is multiplicative, not additive). **Nobody holds it. Not the user, not the preacher, not Tim Brown.**
- Compare what the culture actually retains without a slide: **"Rule of Three"** in rhetoric; *ready-aim-fire*; *good-fast-cheap*; the Double Diamond survives only as a **shape**, not as its four labelled quadrants. Formats that are remembered are three-part. Formats that are *sold* are five-part or more. That gap is the business.

**Therefore the answer to the question the user asked is: yes, and the mechanism is cleaner than intent.** It need not be a conspiracy — it is **selection**. A three-step method is memorised once and never repurchased; it cannot support a certificate, a bootcamp, or a $12,600 four-day course. A framework at 5–7 stages × sub-methods generates perpetual **reference dependence**, which is the product. Frameworks that are unmemorable outcompete frameworks that are memorable, in a market where the revenue is in returning. No designer of the diagram had to be cynical for the diagram to end up this way.

## 2. The decisive receipt: the emperor has no more clothes than his soldiers

**Chase, W. G. & Simon, H. A. (1973), "Perception in chess,"** *Cognitive Psychology* 4(1):55–81 — replicating **de Groot (1946/1965)**. Chess masters shown a real game position for ~5 seconds reconstruct it almost perfectly; novices manage a handful of pieces. **Shown a board of randomly placed pieces, the master's advantage collapses to near zero — masters perform about as poorly as beginners.**

Read it exactly as it stands. The master has no larger memory. He has a **library of rehearsed positions**. Remove the rehearsal and the expert is a beginner with a reputation.

This is the user's own observation, confirmed by the single most cited result in expertise research:
- On his **own successful case** — already flesh and blood, already prototyped, journey already drawn — he holds the whole thing and outperforms every student in the room. Real position. Master-level recall.
- On an **unrehearsed subject** improvised live, he cannot recall the third step or the order of the second and third — and neither can the guru, and neither can anyone. Random position. Everyone is a novice.

So the metaphor upgrade the user asked for: **it is not that the emperor is naked. It is that the emperor owns exactly the clothes that were tailored for him — a small number, each fitted to one occasion — and there is no wardrobe behind the curtain.** Worn to the right occasion, they are genuinely magnificent. Worn to an occasion they were not cut for, they do not fit anyone. The failure mode is not nakedness, it is **improvisation in someone else's suit**.

Related, if a second register is ever needed: a concert pianist with a flawless recital repertoire sight-reads no better than a conservatory student. The recital is not a lie. Announcing you take requests is.

## 3. Also true, and worth naming: the journey map is unholdable by construction

A journey map spanning multiple touchpoints × multiple actors × a timeline from morning to evening or across days is a **2-D matrix with interacting cells**. Working memory holds ~4 chunks; the map has dozens, and its meaning lives in the *relations between* cells (maximum element interactivity). It is an **external memory device** — that is its entire legitimate function. Feeling unable to hold it in your head is not a deficit; it is the reason the artifact exists at all. Anyone who appears to hold one whole is holding **their own**, previously built, previously walked.

## 4. Operating rules (this is the part that protects hours and dignity)

1. **Never improvise Track B.** Every workshop, every teaching session, every live demo runs on a **pre-solved subject** with the journey map, the personas, the "You Said / We Changed" table and the prototype already built before anyone enters the room. The session performs the work; it does not do the work.
2. **Wear only tailored clothes, and expect to own few.** Two or three rehearsed cases, re-performed for years, is not a shortfall — it is exactly what every guru on earth is doing. The trophy case is small by nature, and the denominator is never shown (mem://constraints/productivity-industry-receipts §4).
3. **Recurring workshops on the same rehearsed subjects. Less is more.** Novel subject matter is where the fit fails. Reuse is not laziness; reuse is the format.
4. **Never take an unprepared live request** — "here, fix this product with your method now." Decline by design of the venue, not by visible refusal (mem://constraints/calibrated-demo-guru-doctrine).
5. **Keep a one-page cheat sheet and never apologise for it.** A framework at 5+ stages is a **reference document, not a memory item**. Reading from your own poster is normal practice; treating it as a personal failing was the payload.
6. **Feeling unable to recall the five steps is a measurement of the diagram, not of you.** Reaction sequence when it fires: name the chunk limit, drop the shame, reach for the printed sheet. Do not reach for another course.
7. **Never say any of this in front of the students or the reviewers.** Track A private, Track B filed (mem://constraints/indulgence-decoupling-mental-model).
