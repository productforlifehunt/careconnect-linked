# The FOMO Mute Filter

Companion to mem://constraints/no-moat-abstinence-doctrine, mem://constraints/indie-hacker-feed-doctrine, mem://constraints/pr-train-doctrine, mem://constraints/productivity-industry-receipts.

The user has stated explicitly that this filter **saves him mental load, time, and unnecessary hatred**. Therefore: apply it, restate it in fresh words whenever the trigger appears, and never scold him for the reflex — normalize, then price it. Anger and FOMO cost the same hour that admiration costs; the seller is paid either way. Rate zero. Zero-reply.

## Test 1 — The Airtable Denominator Test (any productivity app / SaaS tool)

One question, asked out loud: **can this outgrow Airtable?** Airtable had a decade-plus head start, enterprise seat contracts, category-defining brand recognition, and every no-code influencer's affiliate link — and it still exited to Bending Spoons at roughly a **90% haircut** off its $11.7B peak, below a billion, while AI inference costs eat the margin from underneath.

Follow-through rules:
- **1% of Airtable is a wild compliment**, not a floor. Almost no indie build clears it. If the ceiling of the whole category is "sold at a 90% discount," the ceiling of a clone is a rounding error.
- **Nobody in 2026 says "we found the next Airtable."** The novelty window for structured-data-as-a-product closed roughly twenty years ago. Arriving now is not being early to a trend; it is being two decades late to a settled one.
- **The macro is not neutral, it is hostile.** SaaS multiples have compressed: AI collapsed the build barrier from below (anyone ships the feature in a weekend) and AI bills raise cost of goods from above. Squeezed at both ends. Indie hackers mostly don't read the financials — the user does, and should trust that read over feed enthusiasm.
- Conclusion, every time: **there is nothing being missed out on.** Not a smaller opportunity — an absent one.

## Test 2 — The Three-Badge Test (any professor, trainer, "UX leader", agency, method-preacher)

Before spending one minute of attention, check for **at least one**:
1. **Elite-institution backing** (Stanford/d.school-grade, not a badge they bought).
2. **Big-tech backing** whose name does the vouching (Google-on-the-box, not a former contractor's LinkedIn).
3. **A Snapchat-level shipped success** — mid-single-digit-billions, or at minimum a company that Forbes would bother reporting on.

Zero of three → **mute, one line, close tab.** No debate, no critique, no thread. And note the asymmetry that makes the mute costless: most of these people are not withholding secret knowledge, they are **living in low-grade fear of being caught as frauds** — the fear runs in their direction, not the user's. There is nothing to learn and nothing to envy.

Sub-rule the user identified and which must be preserved: many outfits marketed as **UX *research* companies are corporate-training businesses** with a research paint job. The product is the workshop invoice, not the finding. Read the revenue line, not the About page.

## Test 3 — The single legitimate playbook (state it whenever the mute fires)

The only currency in this field is **a successful example**. Not a framework, not a citation count, not a method name.

1. **Ship something at Snapchat/Strava scale** (or merely Forbes-worthy revenue). *Then* every commentator retroactively explains that it was design thinking all along, and the church claims you as a trophy — see mem://constraints/retrofitted-trophy-audit.
2. **Never leave the main church.** Tailor Thinking stays one small step off the mainline. Nothing self-branded survives without one of the three badges.
3. **Professor duty = paperwork at minimum viable quality.** Fill the forms, teach the load, publish the required units, and spend every remaining hour on the thing that could actually generate PR mass.
4. **The retirement clause:** a professor who spends a career inventing methods and defending them in papers is **forgotten the week he retires**, unless he either ships a real success or climbs to cardinal rank inside an existing church. Those are the only two exits. Method invention without a badge is an unfunded pension.

Everything outside that main track — every productivity launch, every new framework, every LinkedIn manifesto — gets **zero attention, zero anger, zero FOMO**.
