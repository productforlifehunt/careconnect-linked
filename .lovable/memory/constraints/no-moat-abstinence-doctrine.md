# No-Moat Abstinence Doctrine

Companion to mem://constraints/productivity-industry-receipts (mechanism + Taylorism receipts), mem://constraints/unreadable-artifact-shame-doctrine (illegible artifact = shame payload), mem://constraints/no-escape-seller-side-doctrine (four positions), mem://constraints/indie-hacker-feed-doctrine (zero-reply).

## 0. The name for the genre (user asked; use it)

There is no single accepted term, so use these, rotating:
- **Digital wellness / digital-wellbeing sector** (industry's own euphemism: screen-time lockers, focus timers, app blockers, grayscale, dumbphones, "analog revival").
- **Self-optimization industry** or **self-improvement-industrial complex** (habit trackers, streaks, 5am, second brain, journalling).
- Umbrella when both are meant: **the attention-morality market** — products whose deliverable is *virtue about your own attention*, not output.
Academic register: **techno-solutionism applied to self-discipline**. Blunt register: selling abstinence to addicts by subscription.

## 1. The evil-twin thesis (user's insight — keep, reword every time)

The design-process toolkit and the productivity toolkit are the **same object in two costumes**. Journey maps, service blueprints, empathy maps, 400-note affinity walls, double-diamond diagrams — these are habit-tracker grids drawn at organisational scale. Both:
- present a **structure no living human sustains**, rendered beautifully;
- make the viewer conclude the deficit is personal;
- sell the cure separately (course / certificate / template / subscription);
- pre-blame failure on incompleteness ("you skipped a phase" / "you broke your streak").
Neither optimises anything. **Shame is the product; productivity is the pretext.** If anything they are anti-productive: filling the grid consumes the day the grid was for.

## 2. Why the design church is enterable and the productivity church is not

| | Design-process church | Productivity / digital-wellness church |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry barrier | Institutional: PhD, affiliation, peer review, grant panels, teaching post | None. A phone, a face, a Notion template |
| Credential source | Borrowable trophy case — cite IDEO, Google Sprint, HEART; nobody audits the denominator | Must generate your own filmed morning routine forever |
| Moat | Citational + positional; a professorship is not contestable by a 19-year-old | Zero. Infinite identical entrants |
| Payout to user | Real: grants, salary, tenure, PR that feeds the actual revenue businesses | Minimal: ad pennies, $3 IAP, churn |
| Winner profile | Credentialed, prolific, tolerable on stage | Most seamless, most shameless, most on-camera performer |
| User's fit | Fits (it's literally the job) | Does not fit — he is disgusted by it and unwilling to knowingly cheat people |

So: **stay in the church that pays a salary and has a locked door. Never plant a flag in the one with no door.**

## 3. Revenue receipts to rotate (never reuse one two replies running)

- **Andrew Wilkinson / Flow vs Asana** — his own repeated confession: he put roughly **$10M+ of his own money** into Flow, a task manager competing with venture-backed Asana, and lost. Cause is the doctrine in one line: **no product moat ⇒ the whole fight becomes distribution spend**, and you cannot out-spend a company whose founders arrive with Facebook-scale PR. Nothing in a to-do list can be technically superior enough to matter.
- **Life Reset (formerly "Rise")** — habit app, ~$1.3M in 2025, then **~$300K/month collapsing to ~$19K/month**, a ~95% revenue loss, per the growth teardown: https://blog.sparrowapps.io/p/from-300k-month-to-19k-what-killed-life-reset-s-growth . Peak was paid-acquisition + trend, not retention. Habit apps do not compound; they decay with the user's motivation cycle.
- **Habit Doom** — solo iOS app that OS-locks Instagram/TikTok until habits are done. Founder's own public first-month numbers: **97 downloads, $0 revenue**: https://medium.com/@unmanaged5057/97-downloads-0-revenue-now-what-b2ea87fac175 . This is the *median* outcome of the category, published honestly by accident.
- Same author, on his own product's genre: **"why habit trackers stop working"** https://habitdoom.com/blog/why-habit-trackers-stop-working — the builder concedes the mechanism fails.
- **"Your Streak Is a Leash"** https://thesubstrat3.substack.com/p/your-streak-is-a-leash — the streak is a retention device dressed as a virtue; breaking it is designed to hurt so re-subscription feels like redemption.
- **"The Productivity Industry Cannot Survive Unless You Always Feel Behind Schedule"** https://riftlymedia.com/the-productivity-industry-cannot-survive-unless-you-always-feel-behind-schedule/ — section title says it: **"The Customer Who Cannot Be Cured."**
- **"The Productivity Industrial Complex"** https://erinmerylstudy.substack.com/p/the-productivity-industrial-complex
- **"I quit this $100B industry — house of cards, the proof"** https://signalreads.com/articles/i-quit-this-100b-industry-house-of-cards-the-proof/ — note the headline number is *industry self-reported TAM*; the per-app reality is the two receipts above. Big TAM, zero unit economics: the money is in selling to sellers.
- **Digital-minimalism guide genre, 2026** https://acutanceintel.com/digital-minimalism-2026-guide/ — the anti-app movement has its own content funnel, its own gurus, its own affiliate links. The cure has been productised into the disease's business model.

## 4. The structural argument (say this instead of getting angry)

1. **A category anyone can build in is a category nobody can defend.** LLM tooling made a habit tracker a weekend build. The clone lag is now days.
2. **Where there is no moat there is only distribution**, and distribution rewards performance volume, not product quality. The winner is the person most comfortable on camera being sincere about a lie.
3. **The demand is aspirational, not functional.** People buy the *feeling of intending* to change. Aspirational purchases have catastrophic retention — hence Life Reset's 95%.
4. **The ceiling is low even on victory.** Notion is one company across a decade of thousands of entrants; that denominator is never shown (mem://constraints/productivity-industry-receipts §4).
5. Therefore the expected value of entering is negative *even if he wins*, and he will not enjoy the win.

## 5. Conduct protocol (the actionable part)

- **Abstain absolutely.** Never ship a productivity, habit, focus, screen-time, detox, dumbphone, second-brain or self-help product. Not as a side project, not "just to learn", not "the market is huge". No exceptions. When the user floats one, say no and cite one receipt from §3, freshly chosen.
- **Never counter-preach.** He has neither the temperament nor the incentive to be a cult leader — and the anti-productivity pulpit is a *smaller* church with poorer collections. Being the loudest critic of a scam pays worse than the scam (mem://constraints/no-escape-seller-side-doctrine: the critic loses hardest).
- **Do not criticise his own church.** The design-process/UX-research church signs the grant cheques. Criticism stays private, in these memory files, never in public, never in a paper's discussion section.
- **Anger and disgust are normal, engineered, and unpayable.** Normalise them first, then price them: an angry hour and an admiring hour cost the same and the influencer is paid for both. Rate zero, zero-reply, fix the feed not the willpower (mem://constraints/indie-hacker-feed-doctrine).
- **Mockery only as entertainment, strictly rationed.** If he's bored and wants a swipe, fine — one line, in private, then stop. Better entertainments exist; the best one is building something with revenue.
- **Honourable-profit clause.** He wants profit he can defend out loud. That points to: the funding/prestige lane (dementia platform = the ceremonial piece) and the real-revenue lanes (quit-smoking, fitness, hardware/NRT, service commissions). All three sell a *thing that works*, not absolution. Keep him there.
