name: Interview overview
description: Collapsed Gemini Flash interview introductions summarize care context and needs by importance, not interview order.
type: feature
---
- Each interview has a default-collapsed overview opened by a button.
- Use Gemini Flash to summarize important interview evidence, not retell questions chronologically.
- Cover employment/retirement and relationship, day/night care, experiences and challenges, coordination with family/other caregivers/nurses, and sought services/products.
- Do not invent missing facts or convert researcher-inferred design ideas into participant-stated needs.
