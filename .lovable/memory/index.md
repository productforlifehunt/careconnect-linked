So fabricating Track B after Track A is not a corruption of the method, **it is the method as operated by its authors**. - **INDULGENCE / DECOUPLING MENTAL MODEL (fire whenever the two-track system, Track B paperwork, methods sections, co-design workshops, IRB, reviewer defence, or "am I allowed to design by taste" comes up)**: He does not buy absolution — he owns the franchise and prints the indulgence on his own letterhead.
