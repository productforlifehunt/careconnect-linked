1. Improve eating and prevent weight loss
In the late stages of dementia, a person living with dementia
may experience difficulty chewing and swallowing. Reduced
abilities to smell, taste and see may also happen due to ageing
and dementia. These can have a negative impact on the
person’s health. Depression and changes in eating patterns
may also impair eating and digestion.

Ling believes that her mother is not hungry and does not
want to disturb her sleep. A recent check-up showed that
