Changes in judgement may include saying the wrong thing at
the wrong time, acting inappropriately in a situation, or not
understanding one’s own limitations.
Usually these actions are not harmful, but they can be
stressful or embarrassing for the person living with dementia
or the carer. People with dementia are often looking for
