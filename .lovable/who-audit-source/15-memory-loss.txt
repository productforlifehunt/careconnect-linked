Why is this lesson important?
Memory loss can be frustrating for the person living with
dementia and the carer.

How will this lesson help me?
In this lesson you will practice several ways to respond
to memory loss, and find out what may help you deal
with the person’s increasing forgetfulness.

1. What is memory loss?
People with dementia will increasingly lose their memory
over time. In the beginning, they may forget recent events,
where they put their keys, their wallet or a particular piece
of paper. They find it harder to remember phone numbers,
important dates and appointments. However, past memories,
such as childhood memories, are often preserved for a
longer period of time.
