1. Thoughts determine how we feel

Your thoughts determine how you feel. Unhelpful
thoughts can make us feel bad.

is to think differently. Let’s look at an example.

How will this lesson help me?

It is not an event that makes you feel angry, sad, happy or
guilty, it is how you think about it.

This lesson will help you think differently and change
your unhelpful thoughts into helpful ones.
