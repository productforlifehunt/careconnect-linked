Why is this lesson important?
Certain changes in behaviour can be particularly stressful
for the person living with dementia and the carer.

How will this lesson help me?
This lesson will summarize ways to prevent, reduce or
manage behaviour changes.

1. The most important things to keep in mind when
dealing with behaviour changes
zz

Behaviour changes can be stressful for the person you
care for and yourself. This is quite common.

zz

Try to make the person you care for feel more comfortable.

zz

Look for ways to prevent or reduce behaviour changes
that are stressful, for example by using memory aids.

zz

Try to distract the person you care for, don’t argue.

zz

When one approach does not work, try another one.

zz

Realize that there may be good and bad days.

zz

Consult a medical doctor to identify any causes related to
medication or illness.

zz

Identify whether the environment or your approach could
be causing the particular symptom.

zz

Remind yourself that behaviour changes are part of the
disease but other possible causes should be identified
before accepting the disease as a cause.

2. Practical tips on not blaming yourself, sharing
your feelings with others and making time for yourself
Here are some tips from related lessons. If you did not take these
lessons yet, you can complete them after finishing this one.
zz

Don’t blame yourself or the person living with dementia
for the problems that you encounter. If you want to work
on ways to manage your feelings, take a look at the last
page of Lesson 3. Thinking differently in Module 3.

zz

Share your feelings about your experiences as a carer
with others. If you keep them to yourself it may be more
difficult for you to look after your family member or friend.
If you want to learn more about involving others, take a
look at Lesson 4. Involving others in Module 2.

zz

It is essential that you make time for yourself. This will
allow you to do the things that you value in life, such as
spending time with others or enjoying your favourite
hobbies. If you want to learn more about doing pleasant
activities, take a look at Lesson 2. Making time for
pleasant activities in Module 3.
