1. The most important things to keep in mind when
dealing with behaviour changes
zz

Behaviour changes can be stressful for the person you
care for and yourself. This is quite common.

zz

Try to make the person you care for feel more comfortable.

zz

Look for ways to prevent or reduce behaviour changes
that are stressful, for example by using memory aids.

zz

Try to distract the person you care for, don’t argue.

zz

When one approach does not work, try another one.

zz

Realize that there may be good and bad days.

zz

Consult a medical doctor to identify any causes related to
medication or illness.

zz

Identify whether the environment or your approach could
be causing the particular symptom.

zz

Remind yourself that behaviour changes are part of the
disease but other possible causes should be identified
before accepting the disease as a cause.
